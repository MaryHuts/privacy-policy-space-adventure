# Privacy Policy for Project Artifact: Cosmic Connection

Last updated: May 07, 2026

**MaryHut** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Project Artifact: Cosmic Connection** mobile application (the "Service"). This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy.
## 1. Data Collection and Access

As the developer of this application, **I do not collect, store, or have any access to your personal data**. The app is designed to function without requiring any personal information to be sent to the developer.
## 2. Third-Party Services and Data Deletion

The Service is built using third-party platforms that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

 **Lovable**: The game is hosted on the Lovable platform.

 **Google Play**: Manages app distribution and basic telemetry.

 **Google AdMob**: Provides advertisements within the app.

These platforms may collect data (such as device identifiers or technical logs) as part of their service. Data deletion: Any data collected by these third parties is managed by them. Theoretically, such data may be deleted upon the closure of your account on those platforms, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
## 3. Children’s Privacy and Safety

Our Service is designed to be appropriate for children aged 13 and under (recommended for ages 9-10 and up). We prioritize a safe environment:

 **No Accounts**: The app does not provide any option to create an account or provide personal details.

 **Single Player**: The game is a single-player experience.

 **No Social Features**: There are no chat rooms, messaging systems, or social media integrations.

 **Appropriate Ads**: Advertisements provided via AdMob are filtered to be age-appropriate for younger players.

## 4. Permissions and Hardware

To ensure maximum privacy, **this application does not require or request access to**:

Your location (GPS).

Your camera or microphone.

Your contacts or files.

Any other sensitive hardware features.

## 5. Log Data

In the event of an error, third-party services may collect "Log Data" (e.g., device IP, OS version, app configuration). This is used solely for troubleshooting and improving app performance.
## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
